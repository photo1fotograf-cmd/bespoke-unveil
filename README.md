# Eternal Script

ULTRA LUXURY DIGITAL WEDDING INVITATION

Create an ultra-premium luxury digital wedding invitation inspired by high-end editorial websites such as Vogue Weddings, Harper's Bazaar Weddings, Dior, Cartier, Louis Vuitton and Ritz Paris.

This should feel like an exclusive luxury experience, not a standard wedding website.

MOST IMPORTANT

The uploaded invitation is the master design.

Recreate it as faithfully as possible.

Do NOT redesign it.

Keep exactly the same:

 layout

 typography

 names

 wording

 colors

 spacing

 hierarchy

 icons

 gold border

 paper proportions

It should look like the physical invitation has come to life.

CINEMATIC INTRO

When the website opens, display a luxurious matte ivory background with a subtle handmade paper texture.

Tiny floating golden dust particles move almost imperceptibly.

Soft cinematic lighting.

No loud animations.

No glitter.

No confetti.

No hearts.

Everything must feel calm, elegant and expensive.

Music is optional and muted by default.

A small elegant music icon allows guests to enable music.

ENVELOPE EXPERIENCE

A handcrafted ivory envelope with gold foil embossing slowly slides into the center.

The envelope casts a realistic shadow.

After a short pause:

The wax seal gently cracks.

The flap slowly opens.

The invitation begins emerging from the envelope.

The paper moves naturally, as if someone is carefully pulling it out.

Very subtle rotation.

Soft depth of field.

Realistic shadows.

Tiny floating gold particles.

The animation should last approximately 6–7 seconds.

SIGNATURE WOW MOMENT

When the invitation is completely outside the envelope:

Everything pauses for one second.

A warm cinematic light slowly travels diagonally across the invitation.

The gold foil border reflects beautifully.

Then a very soft light passes directly over the names:

Milona i Ilija

The names become slightly brighter for less than one second.

The rest of the invitation remains unchanged.

At the same time:

 tiny floating particles drift upward

 background becomes slightly darker

 subtle 2–3% camera zoom toward the names

 elegant paper shadow deepens

After two seconds everything returns naturally.

Scrolling becomes enabled.

The effect should feel emotional and luxurious, never flashy.

FINAL EXPERIENCE

The website should feel like receiving an invitation from one of the world's most luxurious wedding planners.

The guest should have the impression that they are opening an exclusive handcrafted invitation made with fine paper and gold foil, presented through a cinematic, editorial digital experience worthy of a luxury magazine or fashion house.

The invitation itself remains the centerpiece throughout the entire experience, with every animation serving only to enhance its elegance—not distract from it.
Invitation Fidelity (Very Important)

The uploaded invitation is the master reference.

Every visual element must be recreated as accurately as possible.

This includes:

 exact typography

 exact layout

 exact colors

 exact spacing

 exact gold border

 exact paper proportions

exact custom icons from the invitation

Do NOT replace the icons with generic icons from Font Awesome, Material Icons, Lucide or any icon library.

The following icons must be recreated in the same elegant hand-drawn line-art style as the original invitation:

 Groom suit icon

 Bride dress icon

 Church icon

 Plate & cutlery icon

 Wedding rings icon

If necessary, redraw them as custom SVG illustrations while preserving their original appearance and proportions.

The icons should have the same thin elegant stroke, minimalist style and luxury aesthetic as on the printed invitation.
